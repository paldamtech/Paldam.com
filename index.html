Products

What we build.

Each product does one job and lists its prices on its own website. More products are in development.

01 Local government

CivicRecordsOnline

Public records request software for local government. It tracks response deadlines under each state's law, helps staff black out private details before records are released, and gives the public a portal to submit requests and check their status.

civicrecordsonline.com
02 Subscription billing

PayDam

Failed payment recovery for subscription businesses that bill through Stripe. PayDam watches a connected Stripe account for failed invoices and emails customers a short series of reminders to update their payment details.
