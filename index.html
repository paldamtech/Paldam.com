Security

Security and technical information.

How CivicRecordsOnline protects agency records, based on its live configuration. Full details are on the For IT page.

  • Two-step sign-in
  • AES-256 at rest
  • TLS 1.2+ in transit
  • Microsoft Azure, US
  • Protected audit trail
  • Role-based access

Sign-in and access

Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app and one-time backup codes. Each agency can make it required.

Microsoft or Google work-account sign-in on every plan.

Passwords of 10 or more characters, and accounts lock after 10 failed attempts.

Encryption

Databases and files are encrypted at rest with AES-256, using Azure SQL and Azure Storage encryption.

Every connection is HTTPS only, with TLS 1.2 as the minimum version.

Hosting and backups

Runs on Microsoft Azure in the United States.

Database backups are kept for 35 days and copied to a second Azure region.

Agency separation and roles

Each agency's records are kept apart from every other agency's.

Staff see only what their role and department allow.

Audit trail

Every action on a request is recorded, and audit entries are protected from editing or deletion.

Administrators can export the full history, with the records, as a ZIP file.

Privacy

No generative AI. Agency files are never sent to an AI service.

We do not sell personal information.
